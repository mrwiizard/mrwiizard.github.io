Case study

iSmart International

Brand identity and web platform for a payments company serving people and businesses across Africa.

Client
iSmart Ghana
Role
Lead designer & developer
Scope
Identity, signage, web platform, campaigns

The mark

A flowing wave for money in motion, turned on its side to form a lowercase i. Fine circuit lines run through it for the technology underneath.

In physical space

The mark was designed to work in metal and wood as well as on screen.

Web platform

A dark, bronze-accented site with two ways in: iSmartPay for individuals and iSmart Classic for businesses. The hero uses the cowrie shell, West Africa's original currency.

Campaigns

One campaign for market traders, one for enterprise.
